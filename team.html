Theteam is a mix of investors and operators who are obsessed with consumer culture and the brands that shape it.

Dame Natalie Massenet is a Managing Partner at Imaginary, which she co-founded in 2018. As Managing Partner, Natalie leads investments across stages and categories, as well as sets the overall strategic direction of the firm. Prior to Imaginary, Natalie was the founder of Net-a-Porter, a fashion ecommerce retailer which she scaled to over $1B in annual revenue. She was also previously nonexecutive co-chairman of Farfetch, as well as chairman of the British Fashion Council. She is a member of the Business of Fashion 500. She was made Dame Commander of the Order of the British Empire in 2016.

At work, Natalie’s interests include fashion and apparel, luxury, hospitality, and brands that own their categories singularly. Outside the office, Natalie keeps a close pulse on international real estate, loves seeing live music, and hosts the most spectacular and carefully planned dinner parties. Her superpowers are her relentless optimism and unbounded futuristic thinking.
